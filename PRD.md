Version 1.0 | Status: Draft | Date: January 25, 2026

PART 1: FOUNDATION
Product Name
SteerClear
Product Vision
To transform exam preparation from a chaotic journey of resource-hopping and deadline-chasing into a guided, inevitable path where students execute with confidence and arrive at their goals on time.
One-Line Positioning
SteerClear is a personalized execution engine that eliminates decision fatigue by showing students one actionable task at a time—turning preparation from overwhelming to inevitable.

Problem Statement
Students preparing for competitive exams face three critical gaps:
The Planning Gap

Students spend approximately 40% of their preparation time planning instead of executing
The average student explores 7-12 different resources before settling on "the right one"
Decision paralysis creates procrastination cycles that compound over weeks

The Execution Gap

Static study schedules typically break within 72 hours when life disrupts the plan
Approximately 68% of students abandon their study plans within the first month
Guilt from missed tasks creates psychological debt that prevents fresh starts

The Awareness Gap

Around 43% of scholarship deadlines are missed due to lack of proactive notification
Students often discover critical exam registration dates 2-3 days before closure
Career opportunities remain invisible until it's too late to prepare

Why Current Solutions Fall Short
Learning Management Systems (LMS): These platforms optimize for content delivery, not execution. They answer "what to learn" but ignore "what to do today."
Calendar Apps: These require manual planning expertise that students don't have. They break immediately when reality diverges from the plan.
EdTech Platforms: These aggregate content but increase decision fatigue. More resources often means more confusion.
Core Insight: Students don't need more resources. They need a system that decides for them and adapts when they stumble.

User downloads roadmap PDF
Browses curated resource library

Free Features
1. Macro-Roadmaps
Purpose: Provide strategic clarity and build initial trust
What It Does

Generates month-by-month breakdown of entire preparation journey
Shows subject distribution by month
Displays major milestone markers
Provides projected completion percentage at each phase
Offers downloadable PDF export of full roadmap

How It Works

Algorithm considers official exam syllabus requirements
Applies learning curve adjustments based on user's stated level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced)
Allocates time proportionally by exam weightage and topic difficulty
Inserts revision cycles at strategic intervals
Maintains 10% buffer time for unexpected delays

User Benefit

Answers "Is my timeline realistic?"
Provides confidence through coverage audit
Shows the full path from current state to exam day


2. The Beacon (Deadline Calendar)
Purpose: Eliminate awareness gaps through proactive deadline management
What It Does

Maintains centralized database of deadlines for:

Exam registrations (national and state-level competitive exams)
Scholarship applications (government and private)
College admissions
Career opportunity windows


Sends multi-tier alerts: 30 days, 14 days, 7 days, 1 day before deadline
Allows one-click export to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook
Supports user-added custom deadlines

How It Works

Deadline data sourced from official government portals and verified community submissions
Push notifications sent at user's preferred time (default 9 AM local time)
Smart scheduling respects user's time zone
Updates within 24 hours of official announcements

User Benefit

Zero missed deadlines due to lack of awareness
Peace of mind knowing opportunities won't slip by
Automatic tracking without manual calendar maintenance


3. ClearSource Library
Purpose: Eliminate resource selection paralysis through expert curation
What It Does

Provides access to "Gold Standard" free resources that meet strict quality criteria:

Accuracy verified against official syllabi
Covers 80%+ of subtopic requirements
Clear explanations with examples
Free and publicly available
Updated within last 24 months


Includes YouTube channels/playlists, PDF notes, practice question banks, official documentation
Organized hierarchically: Exam → Subject → Topic → Resource
Features tag-based filtering (Video/PDF, Beginner/Advanced, Hindi/English)
Shows estimated time-to-complete for each resource

How It Works

Expert panel reviews and approves resources weekly
Automated daily link validation catches broken links
Community rating system (5-star) supplements expert review
Search with autocomplete for quick discovery

User Benefit

Eliminates hours spent evaluating random YouTube videos
Confidence that resources are comprehensive and accurate
Saves money by accessing only free, high-quality content


Paid Features (Premium)
1. Today Mode (Single-Task Focus)
Purpose: Eliminate decision fatigue through radical UI simplification
What It Does

Displays full-screen minimalist interface showing ONLY:

Current task title (e.g., "Modern Indian History - Spectrum Reading")
Allocated time (e.g., "90 minutes")
Progress indicator (time elapsed)
Single action button: "Mark Complete" or "Need Help"


Zero navigation elements visible during task
Embeds direct links to exact content (video timestamps, PDF pages, specific web sections)
Tasks are atomic and unambiguous (always specific, never vague)

How It Works

System generates daily task sequence from macro-roadmap
Each task includes:

Direct link to specific content (YouTube deep link to timestamp, PDF jump to page)
Estimated duration based on content length
Difficulty indicator (Easy/Medium/Hard)


Built-in Pomodoro timer (optional)
Break recommendations after 90-minute deep work blocks

User Benefit

Complete focus on one thing at a time
No time wasted searching for "which video" or "which chapter"
Reduces anxiety from seeing entire overwhelming to-do list
Clear sense of daily progress


2. Auto-Recovery Engine
Purpose: Transform failure into adaptation—eliminate guilt, maintain momentum
What It Does

Automatically detects missed tasks (not completed by 11:59 PM)
Redistributes missed content across next 7 days
Respects daily capacity limits (never exceeds user's available hours + 15%)
Maintains 10% buffer for future disruptions
Shows visual comparison of original vs. updated plan

How It Works

Runs nightly at 12:30 AM local time
Algorithm redistributes based on:

Remaining days until exam
User's daily available hours
Already-scheduled tasks
Priority rankings (exam weightage, user-flagged weaknesses)


If recovery impossible without unhealthy hours (>10 hrs/day), suggests timeline extension
Presents one-tap approval: "Looks good" or "I need to adjust my availability"

User Benefit

No manual rescheduling when plans break
Eliminates guilt from missed tasks
Maintains realistic, achievable daily workload
Prevents plan abandonment after disruptions


3. Precision Mapping
Purpose: Eliminate navigation friction—get to content in under 10 seconds
What It Does

Provides deep links to exact content location:

Videos: Opens at specific timestamp (e.g., youtube.com/watch?v=abc&t=847s)
PDFs: Jumps to exact page number
Web articles: Scrolls to relevant paragraph


Includes backup resource link in case primary is unavailable
Shows "Resume" feature if user exits mid-task (remembers timestamp/page)
Displays estimated completion time and difficulty rating

How It Works

Automated daily link validation catches broken links
If primary link fails, automatically swaps to backup resource with user notification
YouTube API extracts chapter data for precise linking
PDF rendering jumps directly to specified page
User reporting: "Link not working?" triggers immediate curator review

User Benefit

Zero time wasted searching through 3-hour videos
No frustration from broken or outdated links
Immediate access to exact content needed
Seamless experience maintains focus and momentum


4. Weakness Calibration
Purpose: Personalize time allocation to maximize improvement in struggling areas
What It Does

Detects weak subjects through:

User's explicit confidence ratings (1-5 scale)
Task completion patterns (tasks consistently taking >120% of allocated time)
"Need Help" frequency by subject
Post-task difficulty ratings


Automatically allocates more time to weak subjects (1.3×–1.5× base time)
Reduces time on strong subjects (0.8× base time for maintenance)
Recalibrates weekly based on progress signals

How It Works

Algorithm applies weakness multipliers:

Strong subjects (rated 5): 0.8× time
Average subjects (rated 3-4): 1.0× time
Weak subjects (rated 1-2): 1.3×–1.5× time


Extra time achieved through:

More frequent micro-sessions (spaced repetition)
Compression of strong subjects
Timeline extension if needed


Dashboard shows time allocation pie chart with explanations
User can manually override if they disagree

User Benefit

Systematic improvement in struggling areas
No guesswork about how much extra time to allocate
Transparent reasoning behind schedule adjustments
Faster overall improvement through focused effort

Assumptions and Risks
Critical Assumptions
Market Assumptions

Students actively experience decision fatigue during exam prep (validated informally, needs ongoing validation)
Users are willing to pay ₹299-1,199 for execution assistance (pricing assumption)
Free resource quality is sufficient for exam success (no need to create original content)
Target market has smartphone access and reliable internet for app usage
UPI is acceptable payment method for target demographic (aged 18-28)

Product Assumptions

Rule-based roadmap generation is "good enough" without ML (simplicity over sophistication)
Single-task focus actually reduces anxiety rather than feeling restrictive
Users will trust curated free resources over paid courses
Auto-recovery algorithm produces acceptable plans without human review
Mobile-first experience is sufficient (desktop not critical for MVP)

Business Assumptions

Word-of-mouth can drive growth without significant paid marketing spend
Solo founder or small team can manually curate resources at launch scale
Payment processing fees (2-3% UPI) are sustainable
Churn after time-limited access expiry is acceptable (repurchase model works)


Product Risks
Risk 1: Low Free-to-Paid Conversion

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High (threatens business viability)
Mitigation:

Test multiple paywall timings (Day 3 vs Day 5 vs Day 7)
A/B test paywall messaging variants
Offer 3-day trial of Today Mode before paywall
User interviews to understand conversion blockers
Monitor "why not upgrade?" survey responses



Risk 2: Auto-Recovery Produces Poor Plans

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High (core value proposition fails)
Mitigation:

Extensive edge case testing before launch (50+ scenarios)
Manual override always available
"Report issue with plan" button for rapid feedback
Conservative redistribution (under-promise, over-deliver)
Weekly algorithm tuning based on user acceptance rates



Risk 3: Resource Link Degradation

Likelihood: High (YouTube videos get deleted, PDFs move)
Impact: Medium (damages trust, creates friction)
Mitigation:

Backup resource for every primary link
Weekly manual link validation sweep
User reporting: "Link broken?" with 24-hour response SLA
Partner with stable content creators for long-term hosting
Maintain local PDF copies where legally permissible



Risk 4: Users Don't Complete Onboarding

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: Medium (limits activation)
Mitigation:

Onboarding flow takes <90 seconds
Smart defaults pre-filled (most common exam, 6 hours/day)
Skip options for non-critical questions
Progress indicator shows "almost done"
Exit intent: "Your roadmap is 30 seconds away"




Market Risks
Risk 1: Low Awareness of "Execution Problem"

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: Medium (slower adoption curve)
Mitigation:

Content marketing educates on decision fatigue
Landing page directly addresses "planning vs doing" gap
Free tier lets users experience the problem firsthand
Testimonials highlight "before SteerClear chaos"
SEO targets "study plan not working" type queries



Risk 2: Established Players Add Similar Features

Likelihood: High (within 12-18 months if successful)
Impact: High (competitive pressure)
Mitigation:

Speed of execution (launch fast, iterate faster)
Own "execution" positioning vs "learning" category
Focus on simplicity (competitors add features, we subtract noise)
Build curation quality moat (hard to replicate manually vetted library)
User habit lock-in through daily engagement



Risk 3: Payment Friction (UPI-only)

Likelihood: Low-Medium
Impact: Medium (limits conversion)
Mitigation:

UPI is widely adopted in target demographic (India, age 18-28)
Clear instructions for first-time UPI users
Monitor payment abandonment rate
Add card payments if UPI friction exceeds 20% drop-off
Partner with payment gateway offering lowest fees




Operational Risks
Risk 1: Manual Curation Doesn't Scale

Likelihood: High (as user base grows)
Impact: Medium (quality degradation)
Mitigation:

Start with top 15 exams (focused scope)
Community submissions with moderator approval
Document curation standards rigorously
Hire part-time curators at 1,000+ paid users
Automated first-pass quality filters



Risk 2: Solo Founder Bandwidth Limits

Likelihood: High (inevitable for solo projects)
Impact: High (slows growth and support)
Mitigation:

Ruthless prioritization (features that move North Star metric only)
Automate repetitive tasks (notifications, link validation)
Use no-code/low-code where possible (payment flows, forms)
Community moderators for content flagging
Plan for first hire at ₹2,00,000 monthly revenue



Risk 3: Server/Infrastructure Costs Exceed Revenue

Likelihood: Low (given free tier options)
Impact: Medium (unsustainable unit economics)
Mitigation:

Use free tiers: Firebase (database, auth), Vercel (hosting), Cloudflare (CDN)
Optimize database queries for efficiency
Cache aggressively (roadmaps, resources)
Monitor costs weekly
Plan infrastructure scaling at 5,000+ users



Risk 4: Deadline Database Becomes Outdated

Likelihood: Medium (manual updates are labor-intensive)
Impact: High (core free feature fails)
Mitigation:

Start with 50 high-impact recurring deadlines
Monthly manual review cycle for top deadlines
Community reporting: "Deadline changed" button
Partner with exam-focused Telegram/WhatsApp groups for crowdsourced updates
Clearly label deadline source and last update time
